## The 5 Attribution Models (MA-02)

| Model | How Credit Is Distributed | Best For |
|-------|--------------------------|---------|
| **First Touch** | 100% to the first marketing touch | Measuring brand awareness channel effectiveness |
| **Last Touch** | 100% to the touch just before opportunity creation | Quick close deals; direct response |
| **Linear** | Equal credit to every touch | Understanding total channel involvement |
| **Time Decay** | More credit to touches closer to opportunity creation (30-day half-life) | Late-stage nurture effectiveness |
| **U-Shaped** ⭐ | 40% first + 40% conversion (SAL) + 20% middle | **Recommended default** — balances first impression and lead conversion |

**Why U-shaped is preferred**: Reflects Core Dynamics' enterprise sales cycle where both initial demand creation (LinkedIn, content) and conversion events (SDR sequences, demos) matter.

## Understanding Attribution Coverage

- **Attributed**: Touch has UTM parameters linking it to a specific channel/campaign
- **Unattributed**: Touch lacks UTM parameters — shown as `channel = 'unattributed'`
- **Current coverage**: ~70% attributed; ~30% unattributed (contact Niamh Collins for UTM status)
- **Impact**: ~30% of touches cannot be attributed to specific campaigns. Channel totals may be understated.

## MA-03 — Sourced vs. Influenced

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Marketing-Sourced** | The first touch in the entire journey was a marketing touch (not SDR). Marketing generated the lead. |
| **Marketing-Influenced** | At least one marketing touch occurred in the 180 days before opportunity creation. Sales may have sourced it, but marketing contributed. |
| **Sales-Sourced** | First touch was an SDR/outreach touch. No prior marketing touch. |

---

## 180-Day Influence Window

All attribution in MA-02 and MA-03 only counts touches that occurred **within 180 days before the opportunity was created**.

- Touches older than 180 days are excluded from attribution
- This was confirmed with Rachel Summers (vs. 90-day HubSpot default)
- Reflects Core Dynamics' ~4-month average enterprise sales cycle

## Common Questions

**Q: Why does LinkedIn look different in U-shaped vs. last-touch?**
LinkedIn tends to be a first-touch and early-journey channel. Last-touch understates it because leads from LinkedIn often close via SDR follow-up (which gets 100% credit in last-touch). U-shaped gives LinkedIn proper first-touch credit.

**Q: Why is some pipeline "unattributed"?**
~30% of marketing touches lack UTM parameters. These touches show as channel = "unattributed" and contribute to pipeline, but we can't say which specific campaign. Niamh Collins is implementing UTM enforcement to improve this.

**Q: When will the LTV:CAC ratio in MA-03 be populated?**
